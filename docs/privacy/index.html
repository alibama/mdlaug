mDLAUG Repair · browser extension

Privacy policy

mDLAUG Repair fixes accessibility problems on the page you are reading. It does that inside your browser. It has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising, and it does not sell or share data.

What stays on your device

  • Page repairs. The extension reads the page you have open so it can add missing labels, fix dialogs and re-present content. This happens locally and nothing about the page is sent anywhere.
  • Document conversion. When you convert a PDF or Word file linked from a page, the extension downloads that file from the site that hosts it and converts it in your browser.
  • Settings and saved assessments. These are kept in your browser's extension storage and an in-browser database. Uninstalling the extension removes them.

What leaves your device, and only if you choose it

  • Sharing assessments with the mDLAUG project database. This is off by default. If you turn it on (Options → Assessment storage → “Share with the mDLAUG project database”), each assessment you save is sent to the project's research database: the library page's address and title, your scores and notes, any evidence screenshots you attach, the assessor name you enter, the extension version and your browser's user-agent string. Shared assessments are published in the open analytics app, so do not enter anything you would not want public. Switch back to “This browser (local)” at any time to stop sharing.
  • Services you configure yourself. If you enter the address of your own image-description, transcription or database service in Options, the extension sends the relevant content to that address. Those services are yours, and their own policies apply.

Permissions, and why

  • Read and change data on websites: to repair any digital-library page you visit, and to fetch linked documents for conversion.
  • activeTab and scripting: to run repairs and the bundled converters on the tab you are using, and to take evidence screenshots when you ask for them.
  • storage: to remember your settings and saved assessments.

Contact

Questions or requests (for example, to remove an assessment you shared): open an issue at github.com/alibama/mdlaug/issues.

The source code is public at github.com/alibama/mdlaug.
